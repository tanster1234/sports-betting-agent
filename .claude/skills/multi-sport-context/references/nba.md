# NBA

## Market
- The most efficient basketball market: closing sides and totals are very hard to beat; the
  softer spots are openers after news, props, and alternative/period markets.
- Hold ~4.5% on sides/totals at standard -110; props higher.

## Numbers to use
| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Home-court advantage | ~2–2.5 pts (post-2020); altitude teams (DEN, UTA) ~+1 extra |
| SD of margin vs closing spread | ~12 |
| SD of total vs closing total | ~18–20 |
| Back-to-back (2nd night) | ~1–2 pts; larger with travel or a star resting |
| Star absence | MVP-level ~4–7 pts; typical All-Star ~2.5–4; solid starter ~1–2 |

## What moves lines
1. **Injury and rest news timing.** Teams must file injury reports the afternoon before (5 p.m.
   local) with updates through game day; the edge is being early with *confirmed* information,
   not guessing. The league's player participation rules (since 2023) limit healthy star rest in
   national-TV and many other games — rest is less random than it was.
2. **Usage redistribution** when stars sit: props for the next options move more than sides.
3. **Pace × efficiency for totals**; regression of hot/cold 3-point shooting (team 3P% over a
   week is mostly noise).
4. **Schedule density** (4 games in 6 nights, long road trips) — small, mostly priced.

## Red flags
- Questionable star with a decision expected after you bet.
- Late-season games with seeding locked or tanking incentives (rest and minutes become erratic).
- NBA Cup / play-in / playoff rotation changes (shorter benches, star minutes up).
- Totals bets right after a referee-crew narrative with no numbers behind it.

## Data
ESPN (`fetch espn-scoreboard --league nba`), official injury report, stats.nba.com
(browser headers), Basketball-Reference (rate-limited), Cleaning the Glass / Dunks & Threes
(advanced, some paid), The Odds API `basketball_nba`.
