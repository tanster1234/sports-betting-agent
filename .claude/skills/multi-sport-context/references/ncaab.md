# College basketball (NCAAB / WNCAAB)

## Market
- 350+ Division I teams: lines on small-conference games are softer and limits lower; power
  conference and tournament games are efficient.
- Efficiency/tempo ratings (KenPom, Bart Torvik — the latter free) underpin most sharp numbers.

## Numbers to use
| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Home-court advantage | ~3 pts on average; elite environments ~+1–1.5 more; neutral sites 0 |
| SD of margin vs closing spread | ~10–11 |
| Possessions per game | ~65–70 (men); tempo matchups drive totals |

## Inputs that matter
1. Adjusted offensive/defensive efficiency and tempo (opponent-adjusted), not records.
2. **Rotation depth and foul trouble risk** for thin teams; injuries are poorly reported — beat
   writers matter.
3. **Schedule**: conference tournaments (3 games in 3–4 days), travel, exam breaks.
4. **Luck regression**: close-game records and opponent 3P% regress.

## March
- Seeds are not ratings; the market prices ratings. "12 over 5" folklore (~35% historically) is
  roughly what lines imply — not an edge by itself.
- Neutral-site games: local crowd effects are small but real for nearby teams.

## Red flags
- Small-conference games with late news you can't verify.
- College player props: banned in many states; integrity scrutiny is high (the NCAA asked
  regulators to remove prop betting in Jan 2026). Avoid recommending them.
