# College football (NCAAF)

## Market
- Huge slates (60+ games a week) mean softer lines in smaller conferences and earlier in the
  week; marquee games are as efficient as the NFL by kickoff.
- Talent gaps make big spreads (20–40 points) common; σ of margin ~15–16, larger in mismatches.

## Numbers to use
| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Home-field advantage | ~2.5–4 pts on average; strongest venues ~1–2 more; neutral sites 0 |
| Key numbers | 3 and 7 still matter, less than in the NFL |

## Inputs that matter
1. **Quarterback** and **roster churn** — the transfer portal and NIL make preseason priors stale;
   update quickly on early-season efficiency (opponent-adjusted).
2. **Opt-outs and coaching changes** for bowl games — confirm who is playing; motivation and
   rosters vary wildly in December.
3. **Weather** late in the season (wind as in the NFL).
4. Opponent-adjusted efficiency (SP+, FPI, EPA-based ratings) over records and polls.

## Red flags
- Bowl games without confirmed opt-out lists.
- Rivalry/"upset alert" narratives without numbers.
- Brand-name bias in the opening line is often *already* corrected by sharp early-week bets.
- Several states ban college player props (e.g. OH, PA, NY, MA, MD, AZ, CO, FL, IL, LA, OR, TN,
  VA per 2026 reports; lists vary) — check legality before discussing them.
