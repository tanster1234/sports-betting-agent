## Labor and rules

- 2020 CBA expired Oct 31, 2025 → extensions to Nov 30 and Jan 9 → status quo period → verbal
  deal Mar 18, 2026; players ratified Mar 23, owners Mar 24. **No games lost.**
- New CBA: 7 years (2026–2032; opt-out after year 6 reported). Salaries tied to revenue
  (~20% of gross over the deal). Cap $7.0M in 2026 (from ~$1.5M). Supermax $1.4M, max $1.19M;
  minimum $270–300K; average ~$583K.
- Rosters: **12 required players** (no 11-player option) **+ up to 2 development players**
  (cap-exempt; activation limits). Hardship signings when < 10 available.
- Schedule: **44 games in 2026**, **50 in 2027 and 2028** (season ends by late November),
  option for 52 from 2029.
- Officiating: no new playing rules for 2026, but a task-force emphasis on freedom of movement
  and less physicality → ~4.5 more fouls per game, record offense (≈45% FG, 87.1 ppg, ORtg ~105).
- Discipline: higher technical/flagrant fines in 2026; post-game upgrades used (e.g., Alyssa
  Thomas Flagrant 2 and 1-game suspension, June 25).
- Coach's challenge: one per game, second if the first succeeds (since 2024).
- Game format unchanged: four 10-minute quarters, 24-second clock, 5-minute OTs.
- Full-season charter flights since mid-2024, now written into the CBA.
- 2027: centralized replay center (NBA's Secaucus facility).

## Injuries and availability notes

- **Caitlin Clark:** played 40/44 in 2026 (22.3 ppg, 8.3 apg; AP Comeback POY) after an
  injury-wrecked 2025 (13 games). Season-long back issue; late scratch May 20 (league warned the
  Fever for late reporting); listed probable entering the playoffs.
- **Napheesa Collier:** Grade 2 left-ankle ligament tear in the 2025 semifinals; bilateral ankle
  surgery Jan 2026; 2026 debut July 22 on a minutes restriction. Unrestricted free agent.
- Season-ending: Rickea Jackson (CHI, ACL, May 17); Ezi Magbegor (SEA, ACL at the World Cup).
- Sabrina Ionescu missed the season start (foot); Kelsey Plum missed 1+ month (medium);
  Aliyah Boston's Unrivaled leg injury re-aggravated twice (medium).
- League-wide injuries reported on pace for a record (The IX).

**Official injury report:** teams must report by **5 p.m. local the day before** a game
(**1 p.m. game day** for the second night of a back-to-back), updated through the day at
wnba.com/wnba-injury-report. Designations: Out / Doubtful / Questionable / Probable / Available.
Starting lineups are released **30 minutes before tip**. Late scratches are the single most
common way a WNBA bet goes stale.

## Market and integrity context

- DraftKings is the league's official betting partner; FanDuel the official odds provider; ESPN
  BET rebranded to theScore Bet (Dec 2025) and ESPN's odds now come from DraftKings.
- WNBA handle grew sharply in 2024 (Clark effect); 2026 growth was more modest (~+10% YoY per
  Caesars, medium confidence). Clark games drew outsized public action (e.g., 88% of Clark prop
  bets on the over at one book in 2025) — the kind of one-sided flow that can shade prices.
- Kalshi (`KXWNBAGAME` and related series) and Polymarket list WNBA game contracts; The Odds API
  carries them under region `us_ex`.
- Player harassment by bettors is a live issue (multiple players spoke out; Alyssa Thomas received
  threats). In Sept 2026 the NBA, WNBA, WNBPA and referee unions asked regulators for lifetime
  sportsbook bans for abusers. **Never help target, contact or blame individual players.**
